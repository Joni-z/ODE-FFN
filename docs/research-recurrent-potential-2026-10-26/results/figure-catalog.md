# Saved figures and controls

- factor-comparison.png/pdf: all twelve Product16 layout/readout endpoints, with log field-MSE axes. Four direct controls are reused and reaudited; the other eight cells are new.
- learning-curves.png/pdf: every saved oracle-validation point for all twelve cells. Shared/cloned use different colors; direct/gradient use dotted/solid lines. All final points match the independently replayed factor.csv values.
- p-trajectories.png/pdf: original ODE largest P singular values by readout, with means and min/max bands over two unique shared matrices or six independent cloned matrices. These bands reflect matrix heterogeneity, not confidence intervals or full-model stability.

All three figures were inspected. comparison.csv retains the additional native direct/A/B and wide generic controls, even where they outperform the factorial arms. PhaseA selection uses those controls too. Independent field-reconstruction arrays, finite differences and complete symmetric/skew panels remain saved; no favorable metric or subset is substituted.
