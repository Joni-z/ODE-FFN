## all-model-coordinate-errors.png / .pdf

Purpose: distinguish conditional own-coordinate error from dependence on the other coordinates for all seven fixed predictors. Sources: independently verified means.csv and shared row bootstrap. Stacks are C and D; total-error whiskers are95% conditional Monte Carlo intervals. Both panels use the same y scale. The hatched S384 control remains direct-readout, not a fictitious gradient model.

Observation: direct ODE has much larger C with similar D. Potential readout chiefly reduces C. Interpretation must remain about fixed learned functions on the Product16 population; the whole model is neither proven separable nor accepted as useful. All original controls are visible.

## mechanism-and-readout.png / .pdf

Purpose: show the declared direct-readout contrasts and the descriptive readout case study. Left: DeltaD/DeltaT percentages with paired row-bootstrap intervals, including negative shares. Its local percentage axis shows the measured values; the50% majority boundary is explicitly stated in the title rather than hidden as an off-axis line. Right: direct/potential ODE C and D at the same fixed five-time population average.

Observation: all direct-reference upper bounds stay below.55%, while own-coordinate fitting accounts for nearly all the readout-associated risk decrease. This rejects the primary coordinate-dependence explanation without asserting why a cold readout change trained better. No coordinate, time or control was selected after results. Both PNG figures were visually inspected; labels/legends are clear.
