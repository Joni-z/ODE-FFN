# Figure catalog

- `all-endpoints.png/pdf`: all seven arms on all three tasks, showing oracle field error, mean squared Jacobian error and sampling SW2. Logarithmic axes are explicit; bars do not share a zero baseline and the numerical table supplies exact comparisons.
- `learning-curves.png/pdf`: every saved validation measurement, including all historical reference curves. Logarithmic risk axes and a common legend.
- `P-trajectories.png/pdf`: all three P matrices for both paired split responses on each task. Full singular and symmetric-eigenvalue trajectories are exported in P-spectra.csv.

All three figures were visually inspected for labels, axes, legends, arm coverage and agreement with the exported records. They plot actual measured records, with no AI-generated experimental data.
