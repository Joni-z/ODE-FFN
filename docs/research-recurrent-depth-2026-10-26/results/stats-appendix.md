# Statistical scope

Training seed is 0 throughout the registered design. The architecture comparison is descriptive. Checkpoints, internal visits, coordinates and time panels are not independent training repetitions. The nine prespecified-for-analysis paired contrasts in paired-evaluation.csv use per-input squared errors averaged over coordinates. Intervals are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 1000 paired resamples of evaluation rows, bootstrap seed 0, conditional on the fixed trained weights. They describe evaluation-sample variation, not training-run variation or familywise significance. No p-values, significance decisions, run-level standard deviations or multiplicity-adjusted discovery claims are made. Nomination uses the original engineering thresholds and the full generic frontier, not these intervals. SW2 uses 2048 samples and 128 fixed directions; slice directions are not independent model replications.
