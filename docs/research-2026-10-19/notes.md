## Primary-source checks (2026-10-19)

1. JiT official model source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LTH14/JiT/main/model_jit.py ; retrieved full source saved as official-model-jit.py. SwiGLUFFN uses fused w12, SiLU gate times value, w3, hidden=int(nominal*2/3), bias=True. New toy block agrees on these operations with dropout0; not claiming full architecture/initialization reproduction.
2. Arthur Stéphanovitch, *Lipschitz regularity in Flow Matching and Diffusion Models: sharp sampling rates and functional inequalities* (2026 preprint), https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06065 ; full HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2604.06065v1, section3.2.1 Lemma1 inspected. Separates learning, numerical, terminal stopping errors with one-sided regularity. Supports standard error-propagation framework under assumptions; does NOT prove an internal P constraint controls a complete Transformer.
3. Meunier et al., *A Dynamical System Perspective for Lipschitz Neural Networks*, ICML2022, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/meunier22a.html . Historical prior-art context, not presented as new frontier. Establishes that dynamical-system-inspired Lipschitz architectures are already an established area; stable block alone is not a novelty claim. Read abstract page, not full proof.
4. Tao and Choi, *On the Role of Strain and Vorticity in Numerical Integration Error for Flow Matching* (2026 preprint), https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06680v1 . Inspected main proof outline and appendices. Closely related motivation, but contains a problematic discrete claim ||I+hA|| <= exp(h*mu(A)) (main Theorem1 proof outline): pure skew A with mu=0 makes left side >1. Exact exponential bound does not transfer to explicit Euler. Our 2D numerical counterexample gives sqrt2 for h=1. Do not rely on its stated finite-step bound or treat it as certified support for our method. The continuous energy identity itself is correct. Any corrected asymptotic bound must retain discretization contributions.
5. *A Lagrangian View of Flow Matching* (2026 preprint), https://arxiv.org/html/2609.00198v1 . Read formulation and early sections. Discusses denoiser Jacobian and moving predicted target; time convention is clean t=0, opposite to our toy. Not used as proof of a new block or of superior image outcomes.
6. JiT paper: https://arxiv.org/html/2511.13720v2 . Existing x-pred motivation is separate from our latent ODE hypothesis.

Screening outcome: prioritize the 2026 regularity paper as mathematical context and official JiT for the actual baseline. Merely recent papers are not automatically reliable; no exhaustive novelty search claimed. All equations in design.md are independently derived or standard identities, with numerical checks; do not present them as novel theorems.
