# What this result changes

The paired P/W study does not identify the P polynomial parameterization as the primary fitting problem. This placement study does not identify a missing movable SiLU threshold as a practical remedy. Neither result proves that width or multiplicative structure is the unique cause.

One distinct unresolved construction is a nonlinear hidden field, rather than a linear field with a SiLU between two local flows. A cubic dissipative field `y' = P y - y^3` has a simple energy bound and an exactly solvable nonlinear subflow. That supplies a mathematically specified candidate, not evidence of FM gains.

Before implementation, check splitting-network and inverse-square-root-activation prior art. The cubic subflow is a known inverse-square-root response; presenting it alone as a novel ODE mechanism would be misleading. A later bounded screen would need an ordinary inverse-square-root FFN, a paired SiLU composition and strong ordinary SwiGLU controls with actual costs. Do not reopen stopped nonlinear-SiLU, state-clock or affine-forcing settings through extra order/width/learning-rate changes. No follow-up training is authorized by this document alone; the standing user authorization permits a separately justified and registered experiment.
