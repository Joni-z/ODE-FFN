# ODE-FFN novelty evidence: status on 11 November 2026

## Judgment

The project has measured task-specific gains and a concrete mechanism question.
It has not established an ODE-specific architectural advantage or a validated
new dynamics constraint. The strongest candidate is a connection between FFN
functional updates and endpoint mode probabilities. It remains a research
hypothesis until the derived response predicts observed drift and supports
a successful controlled intervention.

Research results in the repository end with the 4 November frozen diagnosis.
The subsequent commits finalize Report2 and prepare the professor meeting;
they add no new model or mechanism experiment. Finalized Report2 is unchanged.

## What is established, and what is not

| Object | Current evidence | Novelty implication |
|---|---|---|
| Original Taylor/matrix-exponential ODE layer | Clock, order, exact-solve, spectral and ordinary controls are complete; extra order does not give a general advantage | Its mathematical interpretation and parameter sharing do not establish a useful new bias by themselves. No directly duplicating paper is asserted here. |
| ISRU-D feature construction | Ring8 MSE .009773 versus selected Swi64 .011279, a 13.35% relative reduction; free ISRU is .009998. Product16 favors ordinary models | A positive benchmark result. Attribution to ODE structure remains unresolved because free ISRU also changes initialization and residual placement. ISRU itself is an existing activation. |
| Input-dependent orthogonal frame | Thin-target K2 MSE .000685 versus Swi192 .003095; the same-frame K1 gives .000713 and better tangent SW2. Thick-target sampling regresses | Input conditioning is useful in that geometry, but the ordinary K1 realization captures almost all of the gain. Existing orthogonal-adaptation prior art and the retained controls prevent an ODE-specific conclusion. |
| Shape/mass checkpoint tradeoff | Across the three Ring8 families, centered-residual SW2 improves while sector-frequency error grows from 15k to20k. Pooled SW2 deterioration is largest for selected Swi64, smaller for ISRU-D and very small for free ISRU | A concrete project finding with all ordinary controls. The broad distinction between field-regression quality and sampling quality already has active literature. |
| Boundary-flux response and P sensitivity | Ring8 symmetry gives invariant oracle sector boundaries; continuity gives exact mass flux. A conditional first-order response B and its K1 P differential are derived | A specific candidate bridge from feature/update geometry to mode probabilities. Continuity, first-order response and the rank bound alone are not established as new theorems. B has not yet been evaluated or calibrated. |

The response under discussion is

```text
B_k = - integral_time integral_boundary p_t (v-u) dot n_k
D_P v[DeltaP] = J_downstream DeltaP q(h)
```

The plausible contribution is to make this bridge predictive and useful for
FFN design, beyond monitoring a hidden P spectrum. It is not yet a demonstrated
algorithm. The current derivation uses the symmetric Ring8 geometry; broader
claims need another geometry and appropriate mode observables.

## Targeted primary literature check

These are directly checked primary abstract records, not an exhaustive search
or a full-method equivalence audit. None is claimed to contain our exact FFN.

| Primary work | Relevant result or scope | Implication for our positioning |
|---|---|---|
| [Carlile et al., ISRLU/ISRU](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.09967), 2017 | Introduces ISRLU and the ISRU variant | The activation cannot be presented as newly invented. A useful new construction or mechanism can still be investigated. |
| [Preconditioned Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02337v4), revised 6 November 2026 | Analyzes covariance-conditioned regression optimization and changes intermediate path geometry through preconditioning | Geometric optimization difficulties in FM are an active, developed topic. Our potential boundary-mass/FFN-update distinction must be stated specifically; this paper is not a duplicate of that construction. |
| [The Reward Was in Your Data All Along](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19162), 17 June 2026 | Discusses mismatch between matching losses and sample properties, then uses discriminator-guided RL | Observing a generic loss/quality mismatch is insufficient as our innovation. Its post-training method differs from an FFN constraint. |
| [Competitive Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07055), 4 November 2026 | Uses competing velocity predictions and a winner-take-all objective to improve the quality/coverage frontier | Mode/coverage failures are current research territory. Its objective/predictor intervention differs from our proposed functional-update diagnosis. |

## What would make the candidate substantive

1. **Prediction:** evaluate B on all six frozen fields and calibrate weak
   oracle/model interpolations against actual sector-mass changes. Preserve
   the existing common-bank readout and distinguish gross crossings from net
   flux. A qualitative correlation alone does not establish the first-order
   predictor at the native trained field.
2. **Intervention:** if prediction works, register a matched perturbation or
   functional-update control that changes the predicted mass response while
   accounting for update amplitude and bulk field error. Check actual mass
   drift and distributional quality, not only the surrogate.
3. **Architecture:** only then test whether an ODE-derived parameterization
   realizes the useful control more effectively than ordinary FFNs, with
   initialization/residual placement and compute controls. Repeat beyond the
   symmetric Ring8 example before selecting image transfer.

All are proposed tests. None is launched in this assessment. If ordinary FFNs
benefit equally, the result would support a general functional constraint,
without an ODE-specific advantage. That is a research decision to make from
the new evidence, not a reason to attribute an advantage in advance.
