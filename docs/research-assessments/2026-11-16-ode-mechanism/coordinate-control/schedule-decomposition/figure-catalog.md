# Figure definitions

- **field-and-stage-action**: Upper panels compare oracle field MSE for the
  native, mean-collapsed and reversed fixed functions, on the complete common
  selection field bank. Y is logarithmic. Lower panels show all 30 dependent
  local layer/clock records per model, each a 16-example RMS, normalized by
  native local increment. Black markers/lines show median/IQR, not confidence
  intervals. This figure establishes use and sensitivity, not retrained benefit.
- **expansion-validity**: Median relative error of the fixed second-order
  bracket prediction over the same 30 records as internal horizon changes.
  Shading is IQR. The dashed line is a slope-one visual guide, not a fit or
  bound. Shared-Pnulls are zero and appear in the numerical table, not on
  the logarithmic curve. This figure distinguishes the correct small-horizon
  expansion from an exact unit-horizon explanation.

Both PNGs have been visually inspected for labels, axes, legends and clipping.
PDF variants are also included. Graphs use only verified complete diagnostics.
