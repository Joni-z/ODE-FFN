# Figure purposes

- target-change-calibration: all cases/splits, actual and predicted pooled squaredSW2 changes over fixed lambda. Notice curvature, sign and lambda1 mismatch. No error bars: these are pooled empirical quantities. Determines whether the local account reaches the real intervention.
- paired-bank-predictions: every full-scale bank effect against its unfitted prediction. Notice deviations from identity and signs across the two common splits. Points are conditional banks, never training replicates. Tests whether pooled agreement conceals bank-level failure.
