# Evidence boundary and design rationale

The preceding read-only diagnostic does not select training. Its finite perturbation is inconclusive because a fixed1% step overshoots even in the shared-gradient control. The infinitesimal temporal direction transfers in both halves with either label definition for ODE and generic controls. Product16 ODE temporal versus pooled gradient cosine is about.50 in one exposed calibration half; Ring8 is about.96. These exploratory facts do not establish a low-rank temporal law or a class-specific failure. No fitted gradient, SVD factor or analytic target is used to initialize a model.

A separate prototype asks whether reusing a low-rank time-dependent generator within the two polynomial terms is a favorable allocation of computation. The same extra6144 dense MACs apply to an ordinary SwiGLU control, while ODE stores half as many extra parameters. This cost-controlled question supplies the additional rationale beyond the preceding generic local sensitivity. Rank4 is fixed for this small budget, without a rank or step sweep. All base weights remain trainable and unchanged in shape.

Known prior: LoRA (https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) factorizes weight updates; TimeStep Master (https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07416), March2025preprint, sections3.1–3.2, trains timestep-interval LoRA experts and assembles them in a second stage. Time-dependent low-rank updates are established prior art. Our cold-trained linear-time generator test neither reproduces those methods nor claims novelty for temporal adaptation. The paper's staged fine-tuning/multi-expert results do not establish our same-cost original-P benefit. The original two-solve FFN and its paired generic counterparts remain the objects of comparison.
