# Figure catalog

- branch-components: native FFN energy decomposition, all tasks, models and blocks. Fractions are ratios of summed energies; magnitude alone does not imply utility.
- hidden-radii: full versus centered hidden RMS before each block. A descriptive comparison across the declared arms, including different supervision for SwiGLU128deep.
- intervention-errors: full validation field MSE divided by the same frozen parent's MSE. Color uses log10 ratio; every displayed number is the untransformed ratio. Uniform-removal ratios equal one.
- case-study: fixed Product16 ODE input 0, t=.5, block 1, in the exact centered-state/orthogonal-update plane. Not a sample-generation plot or an aggregate result.

All four PNGs were visually inspected on 2026-10-26. Labels, numerical annotations and legends are readable; the corresponding PDFs preserve vector plots. Figures use standard matplotlib, with no AI-generated empirical content.
