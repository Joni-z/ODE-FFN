12 November 2026. The user clarifies that short resource requests mean
estimating a whole run rather than asking for 24 hours or forcing two-hour
restarts. Use 100 epochs per arm, one six-hour priority allocation, six CPUs
and four data workers. Keep batch 256, seed0 and the validated model recipe.

Published results do not establish that a small number of epochs is generally
enough. Our 100 epochs are a deliberately smaller first-stage budget. Compare
the trained models by fixed-sampler FID before choosing any extension.
Keep the existing ordinary-baseline FID50k threshold below 50; an epoch count
alone does not establish that the baseline is useful.

At 162,770 training images, batch 256 and drop-last, one epoch is 635 updates
and 162,560 images. This budget is 63,500 updates and 16,256,000 seen images,
half of the former 200-epoch target. Other datasets and batch sizes have
different updates per epoch; their epoch counts are not interchangeable.

## Scheduling change

The old jobs 22813322/22813323 were verified pending and cancelled before
any allocation or training. Their checkpoint/output directories did not
exist; no trained state is discarded. A hold request returned an unspecified
Slurm error and made no change. The [replacement receipt](budget-replacement.json)
records the actual cancellation state. Slurm stores scripts at submission,
so the hard-coded old 200-epoch scripts must be replaced rather than edited
in the checkout and assumed to change in the queue.

The new wrapper sets `--epochs 100`, `--max_train_seconds 0` and
`--no-requeue`. It retains per-epoch atomic model/optimizer/two-EMA/RNG
checkpoints and P snapshots for fault recovery, plus GPU and queue monitoring.
The four data workers receive six CPUs so workers and the parent are not
deliberately oversubscribed. Worker temporary files also use the job's local
data directory. Neither the architecture nor training equations change.

At 100 epochs, stop and evaluate. If an exceptional timeout/failure occurs,
inspect the latest checkpoint and actual throughput before a manual recovery.
There is no planned periodic restart or automatic training extension.
