# Figures

- `figures/response-localization.png` and `.pdf`: all seven raw paired risks and residual risks after affine/full privileged out-of-fold fits. The right panel shows the incremental-nonlinear shares of original-ODE excess versus all three direct references, with paired refitted-bootstrap intervals. These are diagnostic residuals, not trained model comparisons after a deployable correction. The narrow percentage axis is labeled and the100% reference remains visible.
- `figures/conditional-cases.png` and `.pdf`: the preselected three coordinates at three times. Dots are binned paired errors; lines are averages of out-of-fold fit predictions within the same fixed bins. Gray bars/right axes show population sample counts. Empty bins have no extrapolated fit. Numerical risks retain all rows, including any outside the display range.

The coefficients CSV retains both folds, every coordinate, all five times and all seven models. The examples were not selected by their final error or coefficient values.
