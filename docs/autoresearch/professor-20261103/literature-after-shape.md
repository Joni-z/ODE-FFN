# Scoped literature checks after the P-shape rejection

These are interpretation limits and attributions, not a reading-list dump or
newly adopted methods. Sources were checked directly on3November2026. Existing
ordinary controls and earlier stopped settings remain authoritative.

| Source and scope read | What it contributes here | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| [Activation-Adjacent Layer Normalization, Oct2026 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21527v1), introduction, Section5.2, conclusions/limitations | Input-dependent normalization can change the represented function; its no-affine finite-width obstruction prevents treating normalization as a free coordinate operation. | Theorems concern shallow compact-set LN/RMS compositions; fixed stabilizers and deep residual architectures are excluded. No guarantee for the proposed softened quadratic diagonal or FM quality. |
| [Optimal Brain Decomposition, Apr2026 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00821v1), Sections3–4.2 | Loss-aware matrix changes require downstream information as well as input activations. Its bidirectional weighting uses Fisher/K-FAC approximations. | An increment moment PMP^T is not automatically the complete predictor's loss metric. Its LLM compression assumptions do not transfer as exact FM curvature or an optimizer prescription. |
| [Muon+, Feb2026 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21545v1), Sections2–3 and5 | It normalizes matrix updates after polar approximation; direction/rate controls matter when interpreting optimizer gains. | Its swept GPT/LLaMA perplexity results do not validate P-weight spectrum constraints or normalizing D features. Current official vanilla Muon remains unchanged. |
| [SVD-LLM,2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07378), abstract only | Activation-weighted spectral reconstruction predates this project; the feature-energy identity is not a novelty claim. | This foundational compression attribution is not a frontier recommendation or evidence of a useful generative FFN. |

The radial-D proposal has its own explicit Jacobian derivation and a static
amplitude control. None of these papers predicts its outcome. The next decision
must come from the full registered baseline comparison, not a normalization or
spectral-geometry label.
