## Decision fixed before training

At5000updates the same candidate must reduce field MSE by at least10% against **each** of midpoint, Swi128, Swi143 and Swi160 on at least one16D law; all other laws must be within5% of their best ordinary control. Complete Jacobian error and SW2 on every nonlinear law must be within10% of the best ordinary control. Dense inference MACs must be within5% of Swi143. No earlier checkpoint, subset of times or alternative metric can replace these conditions.

Separately test the intended integral interpretation at the final fitted quadrature2 checkpoints. Replace only that model's gate evaluation with fixed32-node Gauss-Legendre quadrature, with weights and all learned tensors unchanged. On each of the32 fixed probe anchors, aggregate squared gate differences and reference energy per block: require relative gate RMS<=1% for every block/law. On the full field bank require the field MSE to change by<=1% relative to the original two-node MSE. On each full512-anchor time panel require relative Frobenius RMS of the complete network Jacobian difference<=1%. Also record the64-node vs32-node reference discrepancy on probe anchors. These are finite-bank checks, not global integration certificates.

Passing the quality gate but failing this numerical gate supports at most a discrete tied-feature lead and cannot advance as an accurate integrated-flow candidate. No32-node retraining or quadrature-order rescue follows. A passing lead still needs unchanged longer-budget and fresh independent population holdouts, measured runtime and transfer before claiming an architecture improvement.

Failure stops this operator setting without node count, duration, partition, rank, activation, coefficient, initialization, feedback, width, optimizer or training-length rescue. Report every fixed endpoint and the learned C history; active C alone does not show the curvature term helped.
