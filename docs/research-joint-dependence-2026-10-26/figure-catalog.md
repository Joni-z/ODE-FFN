# Figure catalog

- `all-endpoints.png/pdf`: all10 outcomes, with field, derivative and sampling measures shown separately. Annotated dot plots use explicit logarithmic axes; no truncated bar baseline.
- `learning-curves.png/pdf`: all40 saved validation observations. Historical Product16 trajectories are retained.
- `target-geometry.png/pdf`: exact oracle off-diagonal energy and normalized commutator means on the five actual-data time panels. Late-time near-zero values do not assert global commutativity.
- `time-panels.png/pdf`: all50 field/Jacobian panel records, with every architecture and time retained.
- `P-trajectories.png/pdf`: all six original P matrices for each law, at every snapshot. Complete singular and symmetric-eigenvalue values are exported separately.
- `joint-sample-case.png/pdf`: post-outcome descriptive sign membership and nearest-mean distances, including both empirical target batches and explicit Gaussian-noise qualification.

Plots were checked for labels, units, axes, all-arm coverage and agreement with exported data. Point-plot minor tick labels and long case-study labels were shortened for readability before finalization. No training was repeated for plot changes.
