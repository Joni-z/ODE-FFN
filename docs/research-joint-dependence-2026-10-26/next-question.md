# What the transfer result changes

The Product16 ISRU observation is not sufficient grounds to turn a saturating pointwise response into a new ODE method. Its field and derivative ordering reverses after fresh training on a joint law with matched low-order/radial properties. A future candidate must face both laws and strong multiplicative controls; a Product16-only win will be inadequate.

The exact interaction theorem also rules out a tempting shortcut: scalar clocks can already generate high cubic-derivative rank, and the complete trained networks fall outside the single-FFN bound. Adding clocks or constraining P solely on that rank argument is unsupported. The next question concerns actual useful nonlinear feature interactions and how they are learned, rather than clock count or a common origin spectrum alone.

One unselected possibility for later review is to start from a strong multiplicative field and derive its complete nonlinear Taylor correction, including derivatives of its gates. The existing frozen-gate coupled-generator review and the stopped nonlinear-drift/state-clock/recurrence settings must be checked first. A distinct hypothesis, its real compute, ordinary controls and a single fixed screen would be required; merely increasing the order of a stopped field is excluded. No such follow-up is implemented or authorized by this note alone, and no effectiveness claim is made.

The completed study is a transfer test and mathematical clarification. It does not meet the useful original-ODELayer successor objective, which remains active.
