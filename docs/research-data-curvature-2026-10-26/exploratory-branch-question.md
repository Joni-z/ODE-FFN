# Exploratory polynomial-differential screen

Written after the curvature aggregates and prior local-P symmetric minima were viewed, before inspecting the clock-dependent eigenpair calculation. This is a post-result direction screen, not part of the registered curvature hypothesis.

For a fixed scalar duration a, Q(P)=I+aP+(a²/2)P² has

```text
DQ(P)[H] = a [H + (a/2)(PH+HP)]
T_a[H] = H + (a/2)(PH+HP)
```

Over complex matrices the eigenvalues of T_a are 1+(a/2)(lambda_i(P)+lambda_j(P)). Consequently its minimum singular value is at most the smallest eigenvalue modulus and its condition number is at least the largest-to-smallest eigenvalue-modulus ratio. A large ratio is a rigorous conditioning lower bound, not a complete singular spectrum. A positive lower bound 1+a*lambda_min(sym P) is available only when that quantity is positive; losing the certificate does not prove singularity.

The previous complete-network generator-tangent study found no severe route cancellation and positive certificates at that specific checkpoint. The new local students have different P matrices: SwiGLU-target second-solve symmetric minima reach about -1.89, versus about -0.93 or above on self targets. These facts motivate checking actual durations rather than extrapolating the earlier certificate.

Use all nine ODE modules and all eighty current anchors, both solves. Record durations, every matrix's eigenvalues through reproducible source, minimum/maximum normalized differential eigenvalue moduli, their ratio, and the symmetric-part lower certificate when positive. Do not select a favorable duration or omit a module. A ratio above 100 together with minimum modulus below 0.01 is a descriptive severe-conditioning screen, not a causal explanation, stability result, exact singular-value computation or automatic architecture decision. If the eigenpair calculation supplies no such evidence, do not call the missing symmetric-part certificate a demonstrated failure.

No P constraint, root/logarithm parameterization, rational solver, optimizer change or training continuation is selected by this screen. Those would need a distinct registered comparison and actual residual alignment evidence.
