## Objective
严格从数学和实验的角度出发，消融出一个明显可用、可继续探索的架构。 Preserve this full goal until evidence establishes it; a negative screen is progress, not completion.

## Completion requirements
- [ ] A concrete architecture retains an explicitly defined relation to the original ODELayer.
- [ ] Mathematical derivations state its operator, assumptions, limitations, and predicted failure boundary.
- [ ] Ablations distinguish its proposed mechanism from generic gating, capacity and compute differences.
- [ ] It materially improves the practical quality/compute frontier against SwiGLU under a recorded, matched protocol; it does not rely on a tiny SW2 difference alone.
- [ ] Benefits survive a more discriminating analytic task beyond the original 2D examples and a frozen holdout verification.
- [ ] Implementation, checkpoints, training trajectories, source/data hashes, monitoring and an independently recomputed analysis support the result.

Single training seed0 is the user's fixed protocol. Do not substitute repeated-seed studies or add several simultaneous tricks. The 10% relative field-error improvement and at most5% regression on designated control tasks are practical screening criteria, not significance claims or permission to ignore derivative/generation/cost failures. A Pareto gain through substantially cheaper computation may also be useful, but must retain comparable quality and be explicitly demonstrated.
