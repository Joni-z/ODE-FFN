# Figures

- zero-order-endpoints: exact oracle MSE divided by the better SwiGLU on
  each task. Notice product16 improvement over ODE parents together with
  failure against practical references and transfer controls. This determines
  the registered stop decision, rather than an architecture success claim.
- zero-order-learning: complete retained oracle trajectories for all eight
  arms on the nonlinear tasks. Their role is to show fitting behavior and
  the paired-control comparison, without projecting an unrun longer budget.
- zero-order-operators: P spectral norms and actual increment/input RMS
  ratios for the vector comparisons. The initial P shift can change raw
  spectra while preserving the entire hidden function. Layer averages are
  descriptive; they are not uncertainty intervals or a stability theorem.

All figures are PDF/PNG and use native-audited JSON/JSONL. Plot source and
input digests are recorded in plot-provenance.json. Endpoint data and the
predeclared screen decision are retained separately.
