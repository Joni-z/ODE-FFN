## Internal trajectories and numerical scope

The bundle preserves792 recorded diagonal-layer/time rows,101376 eta/lambda channel rows, and270 checkpoint/block rows for E/R projections. Eta and lambda entries at each saved checkpoint were compared exactly to state tensors. Lambda=tanh(eta) was also cross-checked in double precision, allowing the documented float32/float64 evaluation difference (atol8e-8,rtol2e-7). This tolerance does not affect checkpoint equality or endpoint replay, both exact.

P=diag(lambda) is the latent generator. Conditional G=diag(s(z)*lambda) is evaluated on the fixed training-monitor probes. The stored G and scalar-derivative statistics are minima/maxima or aggregate summaries from those probes; individual per-input G matrices and derivatives were not saved and are not reconstructed as new evidence here. Raw eta/lambda vectors are retained in full. No observed negative scalar derivatives occur in any logged panel.

Projection spectral norms and drift are recomputed from saved float32 matrices in double precision. Static-control absorbed E/R spectral norms are computed using D1=1+.6lambda1+.5(.6lambda1)^2 and D2 analogously. They describe an exact algebraic absorption, not a retrained model. Norm products are not the full-network Jacobian norm and do not establish network stability.

The exponential-versus-K2 scalar remainder discussion in the main report applies only at a common fixed conditional argument q. It does not bound differences between independently trained networks. The eigenparameter-slope observation applies only to logged eta/lambda states.
