# Comparison scope

The fixed training protocol uses seed0. Every model's final20000-step endpoint is reported. Checkpoints are repeated measurements of a training trajectory, not independent training replicates. The five time panels reuse the same512 source/target pairs. Projection directions and latent dimensions are also not independent model trials; no p-values or training confidence intervals are inferred from them.

Velocity error is mean squared error per coordinate, averaged over the five common time panels. Jacobian error is mean squared Frobenius error, without dividing by matrix size. Product16 sampling uses8192 source vectors,128 normalized directions and Heun32/128. Both generated outputs and empirical target references are preserved. Off-well coordinates have distance>.6 from either latent component mean; the cutoff was fixed before evaluation. Sign balance and off-diagonal sign moments accompany marginal distances in the complete heldout JSON. Target negative log density is descriptive; a concentrated/collapsed sample can have a better density score while having worse distributional fidelity.

Rank analysis is conditional on each trained anchored base. Its floor is a pointwise singular-value-tail relaxation, not a bound on all retrained networks of the architecture. Reported floor/error fractions are ratios of mean squared errors per time panel. An omitted-orientation constraint can make this relaxation loose. Full-rank target structure does not mean equally large eigenvalues on typical samples, as the population calibration explicitly shows.

Source/data and checkpoint hashes plus the analytic oracle were checked by the native frozen-source audit. Mac replay passed after source/data/hash verification and reloading all four final checkpoints:20 test field/Jacobian arrays,10 conditional-rank arrays and saved-sample metrics were recomputed. Maximum output and derivative differences were below3e-14. Sampling trajectories and intermediate training were not replayed; see product16-local-replay.json.
