# Which recent results apply to this operator question?

This is a bounded full-text check of relevant assumptions, not a comprehensive survey. The independent-Gaussian posterior identity and candidate operator are derived separately in [covariance-operator-mathematics.md](covariance-operator-mathematics.md). None of the following papers validates our architecture.

| Paper and inspected material | What it establishes or proposes | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [Preconditioned Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02337v2),2026, Sections2–4 and Gaussian proof | Exact linear-Gaussian regression geometry links optimization to the intermediate input covariance. Its componentwise mixture rate analysis assumes known responsibilities. It also proposes an additional reversible preconditioning stage. | The conditioning insight is relevant. It is not a convergence theorem for our nonlinear AdamW model. A time weight or a cap on hidden P cannot be equated to whitening the FM input distribution. Keep objective/path changes out of the next operator test. |
| [Improving Classifier-Free Guidance of Flow Matching via Manifold Projection](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/cai26g.html), ICML2026; [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21892v2), Section3.1/Theorem3.3 | Expresses ideal conditional/unconditional velocities using gradients of smoothed-distance potentials and develops a sampling-time projection method. | The potential interpretation has a direct connection to Gaussian-source FM. It supports checking the derivation, not transferring CFG, manifold projection or its image-generation scores into our unconditional training study. |
| [Flow Matching from Viewpoint of Proximal Operators](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12683v2),2026, Sections2.1–2.3 and3 | Builds an exact proximal representation from the quadratic-cost OT coupling and extended Brenier potential. Its OT denoiser inverts a deterministic correspondence; the paper distinguishes this from independent Gaussian corruption. | Our samples use independent coupling. We cannot invoke its exact inverse/proximal interpretation or manifold Lyapunov conclusion for a hidden ODELayer. A potential-gradient representation alone does not turn our trained map into OT. |
| [COFM](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.06042v2),2026 revision, Sections2–3 | Uses a partially input-convex potential with FM supervision and a Hamilton–Jacobi residual. The HJ consistency formulation specifies the OT plan. | This is relevant prior art on architectural geometry, but includes an objective/dynamics constraint we are not testing. Do not add its HJ term to independent-pair FM and call it a pure P ablation. |
| [On the Hidden Biases of Flow Matching Samplers](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.16768v3),2026 revision, Examples3.1/4.1, Proposition5.3 | Studies empirical target laws and general affine conditional paths, deriving a condition for their marginal velocity to be a gradient field and emphasizing non-uniqueness of marginal-preserving dynamics. | Its general non-gradient statement does not override the special Gaussian independent linear-path identity. In its empirical rectified-flow formula, differentiating the common-bandwidth softmax weights gives exactly a symmetric covariance term. We independently test unequal finite target atoms and prior weights. Do not infer that caching target samples alone destroys our identity. |
| [Basin-Preserving Discretizations of Modern Hopfield Retrieval Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.21304v1), August2026 preprint, Sections1–3 | Relates log-sum-exp retrieval dynamics, relaxed attention updates and energy dissipation; its basin guarantees require specific energy-cell and update assumptions. | Our tied softmax field uses known associative-memory algebra, not a novel log-partition construction. Our free gamma, multiple heads, gates and surrounding backbone differ. Derive the applicable one-step energy inequality explicitly; do not import basin preservation or generative quality from retrieval results. |

The resulting inference is narrow: test a signed-isotropic-plus-covariance **internal field**, with an exactly initialized untied decoder and a static tangent control. The empirical failure of earlier vector gates and relaxed ranges remains evidence against assuming that more adaptive clocks alone solve the problem. Whether the proposed geometry is useful after normalization, time modulation and residual composition remains an experimental question.
