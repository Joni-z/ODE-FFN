All axes start at zero and indicate lower-is-better metrics in the caption.
Panels use different scales and endpoints; there is no cross-task aggregate.
Colors/labels identify methods rather than confidence levels. PDF fonts remain
embedded; a PNG preview is also provided. No generated samples or image edits
are introduced by the report build.

The older `cifar-dynamics`, `cifar-cases`, `matrix-trajectories` and
`synthetic-task-boundaries` artifacts remain preserved for the superseded
November1 draft. They are not included in the current three-page PDF.
